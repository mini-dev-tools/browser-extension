About

Commitment to privacy

Most of our tools was created due lack of privacy in third party tools so our commitment is to protect developers privacy. Simply we don't collect, analyze or sell user data.

Our Philosophy

Developers are using daily a lot of small utility programs imposing security and privacy risks. That also is time-consuming to search and open each tool. "Mini Dev Tools" aims to recreate these utilities in a single app/website/extension that does not collect any of the developer's data. Mainly these tools are free and open-source, all our features are free now and will be free forever. We might charge for additional features in the future to maintain the project. We hope to achieve our goals by making these projects open source for collaboration and development. We welcome any suggestions or requests.
